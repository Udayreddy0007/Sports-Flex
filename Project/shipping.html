Shipping Policy

Thank you for shopping with Sports Flex! We want to ensure that your orders reach you safely and on time. Below are the details of our shipping policy:

Order Processing Time

All orders are processed within 1–2 business days. Orders are not shipped or delivered on weekends or holidays.

Shipping Methods & Delivery Time

  • Standard Shipping: 5–7 business days
  • Express Shipping: 2–3 business days

Shipping Charges

Shipping charges vary depending on the order value and delivery location. Any applicable charges will be displayed at checkout.

Order Tracking

Once your order is shipped, you will receive a tracking number via email. You can track your order on the courier’s website.

Shipping Restrictions

Currently, we ship only within India. We do not ship to P.O. Boxes or international addresses.

Contact Us

If you have any questions about shipping, please contact us at Contact Us.
